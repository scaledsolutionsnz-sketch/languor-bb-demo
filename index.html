Languor, the quiet, restful space

Languor is a boutique, self-contained B&B nestled in the rural district of Carterton, Wairarapa, a renowned dark sky area. We offer a well-appointed private suite in a separate, secure wing of our home, ensuring peace, comfort and privacy.

Languor is ideal for a romantic retreat, two couples, small families, or anyone simply wanting to take some time out and unwind.

You're perfectly positioned for the best of the region: dark sky stargazing, cycling, hiking, boutique shopping in Greytown and the wine trails of Martinborough. A beautiful drive will take you to the wild southern coast, where you can see seals and hike to the Pinnacles, or out to the east coast beaches. The property is elevated above the surrounding farmland, offering spectacular views of both the rolling eastern ranges and the snow-capped Tararuas.

Languor features two luxurious bedrooms, one with a king bed and one with a queen bed, a separate toilet, and a cosy lounge with a TV, tea and coffee facilities and a mini fridge. There's no kitchen, so each morning Ruth sets up breakfast in your suite and takes the dishes away. Takeaways and snacks are welcome.

In summer, enjoy a refreshing swim in the pool; in the evenings, relax in the spa pool and gaze up at the stars. For your convenience, we also provide an outdoor plug for electric cars.

Languor is a place to unwind, recharge, and enjoy the simple beauty of the Wairarapa countryside.

A Haven for Outdoor Enthusiasts

The Wairarapa is a wonderful destination for those who enjoy the outdoors. Explore the region by bicycle or e-bike, discover scenic country roads, or head into the bush for a walk or a mountain adventure.

Hiking in the Tararua Mountains offers spectacular scenery and a wonderful variety of walking opportunities. The Mt Holdsworth area is a favourite for exploring native forest, enjoying mountain views and experiencing the beauty of the Tararua Range. For a more relaxed outing, visit the Matarawa Swing Bridge and enjoy the surrounding natural landscape.

For cyclists travelling from Wellington, Languor makes a relaxing place to stay before or after riding the Remutaka Hill cycling route, a memorable journey connecting Wellington and the Wairarapa. After a day in the saddle, return to the peaceful countryside, recharge your e-bike and relax beneath the stars.

Whether you prefer a gentle stroll, a challenging hike or exploring on two wheels, Languor is a welcoming base for your Wairarapa outdoor adventure.

Check-in is from 2:00pm and checkout is by 10:00am.

How many people does Languor suit?

Languor sleeps up to four guests in two bedrooms, one with a king bed and one with a queen bed, plus a separate toilet and your own lounge. It's ideal for a romantic retreat, two couples or a small family.

Is breakfast provided?

Yes. There's no kitchen, so Ruth sets up a continental breakfast in your own suite and takes the dishes away afterwards. The lounge has tea and coffee facilities and a mini fridge, so takeaways and snacks are fine.

Can I charge my electric car or e-bike?

Yes. There's an outdoor plug for electric cars, and you can recharge your e-bike after a day out. Ask Ruth about the cost of charging when you book.

Can you help with transport to and from events?

Yes, transport to and from local events can be arranged. Talk to Ruth about your plans and the cost when you book.
