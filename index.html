A countryside stay run by the people who live here

Languor sits on a quiet rural road between Carterton and Greytown, with farmland on every side and the Tararua Range across the paddocks. Ruth and Grant live on the property, so someone is always around if you need a dinner booking or a hand with plans.

The cottage is fully self-contained: bring your bags, and everything else is sorted. Stay a night on your way through, or make it a base for a long weekend in wine country.

  • Private and self-contained, sleeps up to four
  • Free Wi-Fi, parking and air conditioning
  • Check-in from 2pm, checkout by 10am
